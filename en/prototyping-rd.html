Service · R&D

Prototype Development and Electronics R&D

We turn a new electronic product idea into a working, manufacturable prototype. We run hardware, embedded software and mechanics in the same team and document every phase with measurable deliverables.

Scope

What Do We Take on in R&D?

Feasibility and Proof of Concept

We test whether the idea is technically feasible with a quick proof of concept (POC).

Requirements and Architecture

We document what the product will do, its limits and the interfaces between boards.

Hardware and Software

Circuit boards, embedded software and, if needed, a PC interface.

Mechanics and Enclosure

Board placement, enclosure, connectors and thermal management, together with mechanical engineering.

Test Plan and Verification

A measurement method and acceptance criterion for each requirement; prototype testing.

Transition to Production

Cost reduction, sourcing, assembly and test documentation.

Process

Four Phases with Measurable Deliverables

  1. 01

    Discovery and Scope

    We clarify needs and success criteria and derive the technical requirements.

  2. 02

    Design and Verification

    Schematic, PCB and software architecture; verification through calculations and interim deliveries.

  3. 03

    Prototype and Testing

    We build the prototype, measure it against the test plan and improve it.

  4. 04

    Transition to Production

    Manufacturability, sourcing and test processes; documentation.

Deliverables

What You Get at the End of the Project

Requirements and Design Documents

Product definition, architecture, calculations and the reasoning behind decisions.

Working Prototype

A testable prototype with its hardware and software.

Test Plan and Results

Measurement methods, acceptance criteria and a results report.

FAQ

Common Questions

My Idea Is Not Clear Yet. Where Should I Start?

We start with a discovery meeting and clarify the need, operating conditions and target cost together.

How Many Stages Does a Prototype Take?

Usually a proof of concept followed by one or two prototype revisions; at the end of each stage we report what we learned.

How Do We Move to Series Production?

Once the prototype is verified, we organize the cost, sourcing and test processes and prepare the manufacturing files and assembly instructions.
