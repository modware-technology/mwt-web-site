System

How Does FIELD Work?

FIELD SENSOR measures the soil, the water and the pump in the field and reports over LoRa; FIELD GATEWAY receives the readings and forwards them to the computer over USB. The local dashboard on the computer shows the values, raises alerts and keeps a log, with no internet needed.

In the field

5 measurements Soil moisture · line pressure · flow · soil temperature · pump state
LoRa 868 MHz Short, acknowledged data packets

At the computer

LoRa 868 MHz Readings from the field
Computer Local dashboard · alerts · time-stamped log

Months on Battery

On a protected 18650 cell, the calculated life at a 5-minute interval is ~6.5–12 months; it charges over USB-C. To be verified by measurement on the prototype.

No Internet Needed

Your data stays on your computer: local dashboard, alerts and a time-stamped log. No cloud subscription required.

Deep Sleep Between Readings

The sensors are powered only while measuring; between readings the board sleeps deeply.

Expandable

Sensors plug into standard JST-PH connectors; firmware updates and tests run over the USB service port.
