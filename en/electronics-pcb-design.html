Service · Hardware and PCB design

Electronics, Hardware and PCB Design

We turn your idea into a working circuit board: we define the requirements with you, design the schematic and the multilayer PCB, then test the prototype and prepare it for production. If you already have a schematic, we also take on the PCB design on its own.

Scope

What Boards Do We Design?

Microcontroller Control Boards

STM32-based control and interface boards: display, encoder, buttons, LEDs and non-volatile event logging.

Power Boards

For 12–48 V DC systems: buck and buck-boost converters, eFuse, reverse-polarity protection and power distribution.

Measurement and Sensor Boards

Current, voltage, temperature and pressure measurement; signal conditioning and calibration-friendly design.

Communication Boards

RS-485 (Modbus RTU), isolated USB, LoRa, I²C, SPI and UART interfaces.

Modular Systems

Carrier board and plug-in module architecture; board-to-board connections and mechanical fit.

Field-Ready Design

Protection against reverse connection, short circuits, overvoltage and ESD; a layout that considers EMC/EMI from the start.

PCB design

What Do We Cover in PCB Design?

Multilayer Boards

We set the layer count based on what the board needs and plan the stackup and ground planes for signal integrity.

High Current and Voltage

Copper pours for power paths, current capacity and voltage clearances per IPC-2221.

Noise and EMC

A layout that keeps switching loops small; separate analog, digital and power areas.

Fine-Pitch ICs

QFN and TSSOP packages with 0.5 mm pitch, thermal pads and via placement.

Manufacturability (DFM)

Trace widths, spacing and via sizes within manufacturing rules; design rule check (DRC).

Mechanical Fit

Enclosure, connector and mounting hole positions; height and interference checks with the 3D model.

Process

Four Steps from Idea to Production

  1. 01

    Requirements

    Together we define what the board will do, where it will operate and how many units will be made.

  2. 02

    Schematic and Calculations

    Component selection, power and thermal calculations, simulation if needed; we present the schematic for your approval step by step.

  3. 03

    PCB and Prototype

    Multilayer PCB layout, manufacturing files and testing of the prototype boards.

  4. 04

    Transition to Production

    Cost and sourcing checks, assembly and test documents; a package ready for series production.

Deliverables

What You Get at the End of the Project

Design Files

Schematic and PCB design files, including the library parts.

Manufacturing Package

Gerber, drill, bill of materials (BOM) and pick-and-place (CPL) files, plus the assembly drawing.

Design Report

Calculations, component selection rationale, test plan and open risks.

FAQ

Common Questions

I Only Have an Idea. Can We Start?

Yes. In the discovery phase we put your needs in writing together and derive the technical requirements; the design starts from that document.

Can You Improve Our Existing Board?

Yes. We review your schematic and PCB and can revise the design, reduce cost, replace parts or add new features.

Can You Do Just the PCB Design?

Yes. We review your schematic, report anything missing or risky, then start the PCB layout. We decide the layer count together based on current, noise and cost.

How Long Does the Design Take?

It depends on the scope. In the quote we break the work into phases and state the duration and deliverables of each phase separately.

What About Files and Confidentiality?

We deliver the schematic and PCB design files together with the manufacturing files. Confidentiality and intellectual property terms are set out in writing at the start of the project.
